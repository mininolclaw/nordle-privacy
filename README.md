    # Nordle privacy policy

    Nordle is a private word game distributed through Apple TestFlight.

    On your iPhone: your games, guesses, stats, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

    Family leaderboard (from the leaderboard build on): Nordle stores the nickname and emoji avatar you choose, an anonymous user id that iCloud gives the app (not your Apple Account name or email), and your puzzle results: which puzzle, whether you solved it, how many guesses it took, the colour pattern of your guesses (no letters) and when you finished. The answer and the letters you typed are never uploaded. This data is kept in Apple's CloudKit database for Nordle and is visible to other Nordle players.

    Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; no server sends them. Nordle has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and shares no data with anyone else.

    Deleting your data: in Nordle, Settings › Delete my data removes your leaderboard records from CloudKit and your data from the phone.
